Case 01

Spring Adventure Camp 2027.

ClientAlice Ferguson Foundation
Year2026
RoleDigital Communications Intern
StatusProposed campaign, not posted

A mini marketing campaign for a spring-break camp at the Ferguson Outdoor Learning Center, aimed at parents of kids 4 to 12.

The brief

Build early awareness for Spring Adventure Camp 2027 before registration opened, and give families a reason to choose Ferguson over other spring-break options.

The research

I benchmarked four regional camps on dates, ages, price ($250 to $595) and messaging. The best ones show real activities and sell what kids gain, not just what they do.

The plan

A three-post launch for LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram: an announcement graphic, a reel built from summer camp footage, and a parent-focused carousel.

Case 02

FlyLine case study.

ClientClass Project: INST367, University of Maryland
Year2026
RoleUX/UI Designer

A seatback tablet app that lets passengers talk to each other mid-flight. Sketched by hand, then taken to high fidelity in Figma.

The brief

I designed FlyLine, a seat-back tablet app, for a class project. The goal: give passengers a simple, safe way to chat with someone else on the plane.

The approach

Paper sketches first, then low-fi wireframes, then a high-fi system in navy, purple and sunset gold, built in light and dark modes for a dim cabin.

The outcome

A clickable high-fidelity prototype and a full case study documenting research and final screens.
