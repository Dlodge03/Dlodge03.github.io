Safety you can see and feel.

A phone app and wearable for people who are Deaf or hard of hearing, turning the sounds that matter, like a car approaching or an announcement at the gate, into clear visuals and vibrations. My part was the competitor research that shaped our design direction.

Summary

This case study contains in-depth research on three safety apps for people who are Deaf or hard of hearing. It breaks down each competitor's features, strengths and weaknesses, the gaps they leave around street and transit safety, and strategies to implement in our app.
